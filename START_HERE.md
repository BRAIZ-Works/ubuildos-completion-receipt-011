# Content Approval Queue — Day 10/30

Version: v1.0.1  
Release subject status: Fresh Independent IQA PASS; owner accepted and frozen.

A bounded operational-marketing demonstration for seeing which content needs review, what is waiting or decided, who owns the next action, and why.

## Inspect locally
Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step, package manager, network request, account, or credential is required.

## What it demonstrates
- a single approval queue across channels;
- search and filters for status and owner;
- visible priority, due timing, ownership, and next action;
- decision rationale and history;
- keyboard-operable row detail and responsive layout;
- explicit synthetic-data and no-integration boundary.

## What it does not prove
It does not prove production workflow integration, identity/authentication, persistent storage, automated approvals, legal/compliance approval, campaign performance, or organizational adoption.
